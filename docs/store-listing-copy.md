Tigers Eye Life is the companion app for active Create Power members.

Turn your program into clear daily action. See what matters today, follow progressive strength sessions, build practical nutrition habits, capture progress, and use coaching prompts designed around your goals and real-life constraints.

- A focused Today view for your current program week
- Guided strength workouts with saved session progress
- Meal logging and nutrition support
- Personal goals, check-ins, and progress history
- A 12-week Create Power learning path
- Optional coach text, voice input, and meal-photo analysis

An active Create Power membership is required for the member program experience.
